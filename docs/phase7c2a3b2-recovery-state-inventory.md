# Recovery-state boundary and mutation inventory

This is an audit and future design, not a journal format or a recovery guarantee. The existing installed summary is a bounded, post-install observation of submit/cancel; it is not a complete canonical decision. All exchange mutations must remain serialized through the existing owner. A future recorder must observe the same owner order, including administrative operations and delivery actions when those actions affect admission.

## Mutation inventory

The owner column means the live exchange owner or gateway owner thread, unless explicitly identified otherwise. Read-only inspection and failed operations do not ordinarily change continuation state; a preparation exception destroys its private candidate. Existing summaries record accepted submit/cancel only, not independent owner operations in the table.

## Recovery classes and proposed ordering

* **A — exact continuation:** all book price levels and FIFO order/sequence, remaining quantities, order ownership/history, request identities, session identity/limits/kill/positions, limits and capacities, matching/exchange/feed counters, audit history affecting capacity, and allocator identity/high-water and publication ownership. Private report queue state must be retained or reconciled before admission because its capacity influences decisions.
* **B — verify after replay:** canonical accepted/rejected outcomes, per-fill executions, resulting positions/exposure, book revision and sequence numbers, owner mappings and allocator continuation. Verification is additional to restoration, not a substitute for A.
* **C — explicit reinitialization:** after old transport sessions are declared ended, create new sessions under a documented identity policy and subscribe afresh using snapshot plus new ordered feed updates. No old connection, generation, public gap or unsent socket buffer is implied to survive. Any mapping to retained private obligations requires a separately designed authenticated identity and reconciliation mechanism.
* **D — intentionally not recovered:** TCP sockets, kernel buffers, thread scheduling and unacknowledged transport writes. Without durable acknowledgments, client outcomes and report delivery can remain uncertain. Do not claim exactly-once delivery or authenticated reconnection.

Recommended hybrid: serialize all continuation-affecting commands and admin operations through the existing owner and journal them in owner order between verified checkpoints; record/report consumption or explicitly stop admission until obligations are reconciled. Restore the checkpoint and replay a validated contiguous suffix with admission and publication closed. Verify state, allocator and uniqueness, then publish atomically under exclusive ownership. Define epochs for new sessions and subscriptions; do not invent a second writer. Storage write/sync, failure handling, identity and durable publication are separate unimplemented work.

## Verification and bounded effects

`InstalledSummary::participant_effects()` reports sorted affected session IDs with before/after command-symbol position, all-symbol open order count/notional, command-symbol buy/sell open quantities and the unchanged scalar risk policy, kill flag and permission for this symbol. The full allowed-symbol vector and other-symbol positions are not included. Exposure scans resting orders and uses checked arithmetic; there is no separately persisted reservation. Final snapshots do not describe transient per-fill risk reservations.

`ownership_effects()` returns sorted internal IDs touched by private reports, with optional before/after owner, client/request IDs, symbol, open flag and, when resting, exact order snapshot including quantity, price, side and FIFO sequence. Closed history does not fabricate a resting snapshot. Neither interface gives mutable candidate access. Counts are bounded by the existing 1,024-trade and 4,096-report limits (at most 2,049 participant IDs before deduplication and 4,096 touched IDs); exceptions occur before installation. The opt-in summary scans existing order history; it is not an additional full-state copy.

For full exact comparison, `exact_continuation_state(max_words)` is a bounded diagnostic word vector: it includes actual IDs, owners, risk configuration, sessions, queues, counter state, book levels/FIFO, revision, capacities and allocator high-water, and validates relationships. It can be compared word-for-word for equivalent contexts; it is not a stable canonical serialization or durable checkpoint protocol. The normalized Phase 7B verifier deliberately relabels IDs for replay, so it cannot establish exact process-wide ID equality. CHRSTATE restores an isolated candidate by replay and validation, not live publication. No digest or proof of collision-free equality is introduced here.

## Remaining canonical-record and storage blockers

Specify bounded encodings for all command and administrative variants, full relevant risk policy, ownership/history, queue semantics and versioned state verification; define rejected-operation ordering and initial state. Establish allocator exclusivity, high-water reconciliation across instances, epoch/identity and failure-atomic publication. Define journal admission/commit/ack boundaries, sync ordering, corruption/torn record handling and storage assumptions before claiming crash durability. Current snapshots, recording and flush do not provide durable recovery or session recovery.
